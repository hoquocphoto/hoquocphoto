About

A bit about me

I'm Anh Quoc Ho, a photographer and videographer from Vinh City, Vietnam, now based in Sydney. I started shooting yearbooks, dance shows and weddings back home, and since moving here I've worked with Fujifilm House of Photography, Porsche Centre Willoughby, G Class Hire and Shein Australia.

I'm also studying a Bachelor of Communication and Media (Honours), majoring in Screen Media Production. Right now I freelance, shooting weddings, portraits, events and brand video.
