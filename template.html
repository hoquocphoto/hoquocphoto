About

A bit about me

I'm Anh Quoc Ho, a photographer and videographer from Vinh City, Vietnam. I started out back home shooting yearbooks, dance shows and weddings. Since moving to Sydney I've worked with Fujifilm House of Photography, Porsche Centre Willoughby, G Class Hire and Shein Australia.

I'm also studying a Bachelor of Communication and Media (Honours), majoring in Screen Media Production. The rest of the time I freelance, shooting weddings, portraits, events and tours.

How it works

What to expect

  1. 01

    Send me a message

    DM me on Instagram or send a text. Tell me the date, the place and what the shoot is for.

  2. 02

    We plan it

    I send you a price. Then we sort out the location, the time and any shots you really want.

  3. 03

    The shoot

    For portraits I'll show you where to stand and how to pose. At weddings and events I mostly stay out of the way and catch what happens.

  4. 04

    Your photos

    I edit everything myself and send you a link to download your photos or video.

Not sure what you need yet? Message me anyway and we'll work it out.
